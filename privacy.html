Privacy policy

Last updated July 22, 2026

What HomeHQ stores

HomeHQ keeps the home records you and your household create: homes, areas, equipment, documents and photos, maintenance schedules and history, expenses, contractors, and shared lists, along with your account email and display name. Records belong to your household and are only visible to people you invite.

Where it lives

Your records are stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) in a private database and private file storage. Files are never publicly accessible; the app retrieves them with short-lived signed links.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your household records with advertisers or service providers you have not chosen. Local provider searches open in the Maps app you pick and send only your service category and coarse locality (city, state, postal code) — never your street address or contact details.

Analytics

HomeHQ records a small set of anonymous product events (for example “task created”) to understand which features help. These events never include names, addresses, document contents, notes, or any household text.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account any time from Settings. Homes only you belong to are permanently deleted, including documents and files. In homes you share, your contributions remain with the household but are no longer attributed to you. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.
